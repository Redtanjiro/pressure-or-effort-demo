# The Blob (public demo)

DDES9154 Wearables & Bio-Sensing Interactions prototype (UNSW), by Neel Parikh.

A jelly blob shares your heartbeat, tells you its day as short stories, and learns with your help whether a racing heart was pressure or just effort. Works on a phone (add it to your Home Screen) and on a laptop.

Four weeks of **made-up** Google Fitbit Air data (heart rate, steps, sleep and stages, HRV, breathing rate, SpO2, skin temperature, Active Zone Minutes, calories and recognised exercise) with example answers and care choices, generated by `tools/gen_data.py`. No real health data is in this repo.

Live: https://redtanjiro.github.io/pressure-or-effort-demo/

**Hangout:** the other blobs in a Hangout room are simulated on your phone (`hangout-sim.js`), seeded from the date. Nothing is sent anywhere. A real version would swap that file for a realtime presence channel carrying only `{ anonId, band, colour, action, ts }`.
