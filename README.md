# The Blob (public demo)

DDES9154 Wearables & Bio-Sensing Interactions prototype (UNSW), by Neel Parikh.

A jelly blob shares your heartbeat, tells you its day as short stories, and learns with your help whether a racing heart was pressure or just effort. Works on a phone (add it to your Home Screen) and on a laptop.

The heart rate and steps in this demo are **made up** (generated by a seeded script). The answers on past days are examples too. No real health data is in this repo.

Live: https://redtanjiro.github.io/pressure-or-effort-demo/
